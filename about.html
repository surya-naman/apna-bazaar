About Apna Bazaar

Our Idea

Apna Bazaar is a college project (First Semester B.Tech, CA1) that shows how small local shopkeepers can sell online. Customers browse products, add them to a cart and place orders. Vendors manage products and orders. An admin sees the overall picture.

How this demo works

Everything is saved in your own browser using localStorage. There is no real server, no real payment and no data shared between different browsers or devices.
